# LA PITAYA — FASE 6: PROJECTS & MISSIONS
## Persistent Project Continuity · Mission Planning · Governed Multi-Work Execution
### Human Objective → Project → Mission → El Inge → CIMA → Team → Deliveries

---

## 1. Objective
FASE 6 introduces durable organizational continuity and multi-work execution to La Pitaya. It elevates La Pitaya from executing single isolated requests to managing complete, long-lived projects composed of multiple governed missions, preserving relationships across application restarts, tracking lineage, and providing natural conversational project status through Alicia.

---

## 2. Architecture
The architecture strictly adheres to the core invariant:
```
HUMAN OBJECTIVE
       ↓
PROJECT CONTEXT
       ↓
MISSION
       ↓
EL INGE
       ↓
MISSION PLAN
       ↓
GOVERNED WORK REQUESTS
       ↓
CIMA
       ↓
REAL ASSIGNMENTS
       ↓
DELIVERIES
       ↓
MISSION PROJECTION
       ↓
PROJECT PROJECTION
       ↓
ALICIA
       ↓
HUMAN
```
- **Human:** Owns the objective and business intent.
- **El Inge:** Owns orchestration and technical decomposition.
- **CIMA:** Owns authority and execution governance.
- **Project/Mission Layer:** Owns continuity and read-only projection.
- **Alicia:** Owns human communication.

---

## 3. Project Domain
`CompanyProject` establishes organizational identity, human provenance, and groups related missions:
- `projectId`: Unique deterministic or generated project identifier.
- `name`: Human-facing project name.
- `objective`: High-level business goal with explicit provenance (`HUMAN`).
- `status`: `PLANNING` | `ACTIVE` | `WAITING_FOR_HUMAN` | `BLOCKED` | `COMPLETED` | `CANCELLED`.
- `acceptanceCriteria`: Provenance-tracked criteria (`HUMAN`, `EL_INGE_PROPOSED`, `RUNTIME_DERIVED`).
- `missionIds`: References to child missions.

---

## 4. Mission Domain
`CompanyMission` represents a meaningful project-level outcome requiring one or more governed work units:
- `missionId`: Unique mission identifier.
- `projectId`: Parent project identifier.
- `title`: Short descriptive title.
- `objective`: Target outcome.
- `acceptanceCriteria`: Acceptance criteria.
- `provenance`: `HUMAN` | `EL_INGE_PROPOSED` | `RUNTIME_DERIVED`.
- `dependencies`: Array of prerequisite `missionId`s.
- `status`: `PLANNING` | `WAITING_FOR_HUMAN` | `CONFIRMED` | `ACTIVE` | `BLOCKED` | `COMPLETED` | `FAILED` | `CANCELLED`.
- `workRequestIds`: Lineage array of generated work requests.
- `deliveryIds`: Lineage array of verified delivery references.
- `plan`: `MissionPlan` generated by El Inge upon confirmation.

---

## 5. Authority Boundaries
- **Project != Governance Authority:** Project layer never approves work or assigns agents.
- **Mission != Governance Authority:** Mission never bypasses CIMA or auto-grants permissions.
- **Mission Plan != Authorization:** Decomposed steps are proposals, not execution authority.
- **Mission Confirmation != HIGH Approval:** HIGH-risk actions always require explicit human approval.
- **Expected != Assigned:** Proposed roles in plans do not set agent states to `WORKING`.
- **Builder != Auditor:** Rosalba/El Beni never act as auditor for their own builds; José Juan remains independent auditor.

---

## 6. Persistence
- Persistent storage is managed via `HIVE_ROOT/lapitaya/projects.json`.
- Atomic writes: Uses temporary files with atomic rename to eliminate corruption risk.
- Schema versioning: Explicit `version: 1` structure with full recovery fallback.
- Restores projects, missions, requests, plans, and verified deliveries across application restarts.

---

## 7. Provenance
Every acceptance criterion and objective retains its authoritative provenance:
- `HUMAN`: Defined directly by the user.
- `EL_INGE_PROPOSED`: Technical decomposition proposed by El Inge.
- `RUNTIME_DERIVED`: Inferred from runtime verification.

---

## 8. Mission Planning
When a mission is confirmed, El Inge produces a `MissionPlan` containing:
- `milestones`: Structured milestones.
- `proposedWork`: Decomposed steps across CIMA phases (`CONTEXT`, `BUILD`, `TEST`, `AUDIT`, `DECISION`).
- `expectedRoles`: Expected agents for upcoming phases without assigning them.

---

## 9. Work Authorization
- Bounded authorization ensures that confirming a mission does not grant carte blanche execution.
- LOW-risk actions operate within confirmed boundaries.
- MEDIUM and HIGH actions strictly follow existing CIMA autonomy and approval policies.

---

## 10. WorkRequest Lineage
Full upward and downward lineage is maintained:
`Project -> Mission -> WorkRequest -> WorkPlan -> Task -> CompanySession -> Assignments -> CIMA Events -> Delivery`
Every delivery contains `projectId` and `missionId` back-references.

---

## 11. Dependencies
- Missions can declare dependencies on preceding missions.
- Dependent missions enter `BLOCKED` status until all dependencies reach `COMPLETED` with verified `PASS`.
- Dependency satisfaction automatically unblocks the mission to `CONFIRMED` / `PLANNING`, but **never** automatically assigns workers or approves HIGH actions.

---

## 12. ProjectSnapshot
A read-only snapshot containing:
- `project`: Project metadata.
- `missions`: List of child missions.
- `currentMission`: Currently active or next mission.
- `activeSessions`: Active company sessions.
- `activeTeam`: Currently assigned agents.
- `progress`: `{ completedMissions, totalMissions, summary }`.
- `blockers`: Active blocking reasons.
- `humanAttention`: Human attention required.
- `recentDeliveries`: Correlated deliveries.

---

## 13. MissionSnapshot
A read-only snapshot containing:
- `mission`: Mission metadata.
- `plan`: El Inge's mission plan.
- `authorizedWork`: Confirmed work requests.
- `activeWork`: In-progress work requests.
- `completedWork`: Completed work requests.
- `deliveries`: Verified deliveries for this mission.
- `blockers`: Dependency or execution blockers.
- `outcome`: `PASS` | `FAIL` | `BLOCKED` | `CANCELLED` | `null`.

---

## 14. Progress
Progress is deterministic and explainable:
- Expressed as `missionsCompleted / missionsTotal` (e.g. "1 de 2 misiones completadas").
- Does not use hallucinated percentages or opaque estimates.

---

## 15. Human Attention
- Surfaces pending human decisions (e.g., proposal confirmation or HIGH approval).
- Directly reflects underlying governance facts without fabricating attention needs.

---

## 16. Alicia
Alicia serves as the primary conversational interface:
- **Questions Answered:** "¿Qué proyectos tenemos?", "¿Cómo va este proyecto?", "¿Qué misión está activa?", "¿Qué falta?", "¿Qué ya terminaron?", "¿Qué está bloqueado?", "¿Quién está trabajando?", "¿Quién sigue?", "¿Necesitan algo de mí?", "¿Qué entregaron?".
- **Ambiguity Handling:** Asks for clarification when project queries match multiple projects.
- **False-Premise Handling:** Actively corrects false completion or false assignment assertions.
- **Zero Mutations:** All status and project queries execute with 0 operational mutations.

---

## 17. Desktop
- Companion seats strictly respect the `<= 3` policy rule.
- Agent states (`WORKING`, `ASSIGNED`, `ATTENTION`, `AVAILABLE`) reflect real assignment facts.

---

## 18. Restart Recovery
- Reconstructs project and mission states after Electron restart from persisted metadata and CIMA facts.
- Never resurrects stale `WORKING` states without an active process.

---

## 19. Multiple Projects
- Strict isolation between independent projects: Project A and Project B share no missions, work requests, deliveries, or progress.

---

## 20. Security
- Context isolation (`contextIsolation: true`), sandbox (`sandbox: true`), and disabled Node integration (`nodeIntegration: false`).
- Preload and IPC expose strictly read-only project and mission endpoints (`projects:list`, `projects:getSnapshot`, `missions:getSnapshot`).
- Renderer cannot mutate projects, complete missions, or bypass CIMA.
